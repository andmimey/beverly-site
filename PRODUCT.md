# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Prospective residential and institutional clients evaluating Bach Architecture's built work; students and academic peers looking up Beverly Choe (Stanford Architectural Design Program). They arrive to browse projects, judge sensibility and craft, and find contact details.

## Product Purpose

Migrate bach-architecture.com off WordPress/Prologic into a fast, dependency-free static site that Beverly can trust. Success = Beverly recognizes her site (aesthetic continuity first), pages load instantly, and design variations can be produced by editing tokens, not markup.

## Brand Personality

Stoic, straightforward, warm. The buildings speak; the site recedes. Beverly's stated favorite architect is Richard Neutra: horizontal calm, precision, warm materials inside crisp structure.

## Anti-references

- Anything that reads "AI-generated template" (Beverly's explicit apprehension): decorative gradients, cards, eyebrow labels, marketing motion.
- Generic squarespace-portfolio polish that erases the site's plainspoken 2016-era character.
- Do not redesign the identity; variants refine, never rebrand.

## Design Principles

1. Fidelity before flourish: the original look is the baseline contract; every deviation must be argued.
2. The photograph is the interface: chrome stays quiet, imagery carries the page.
3. Surgical improvement: each variant change fixes a nameable defect (contrast, measure, rhythm), not taste drift.
4. Token-driven: all variation happens in CSS custom properties and override layers, never forked markup.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG AA in the refined variant (the faithful theme inherits the original's shortfalls by design). Keyboard-reachable nav and switcher, reduced-motion respected, state never conveyed by color alone.
