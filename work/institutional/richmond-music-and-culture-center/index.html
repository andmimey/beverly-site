RICHMOND MUSIC AND CULTURE CENTER
SAN FRANCISCO, CA
A music and culture center designed within a compact inner block space in the Richmond provides a community space, practice rooms, and administrative space for a non-profit organization. A wood walkway leads to the main entrance, reception desk, and central community room. A shed roof captures the afternoon sun to illuminate the individual practice rooms.
