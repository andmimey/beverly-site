DESIGN GUIDELINES

Working reference for the Bach Architecture site. Classic is the frozen replica of the 2016 Divi build; every rule below governs the Light and Dark studies and any future variation. When in doubt: photography carries the page, type stays quiet, orange is the only voice raised.

Principles
  • Fidelity before flourish. Classic is the contract; a study departs only with a nameable reason.
  • The photograph is the interface. Chrome recedes; plates and contact sheets do the talking.
  • Stark, then warm. Structure is neutral grey and one type size; warmth and playfulness arrive only through the orange and the work itself.
  • Flush left, always. Nothing is centered; nothing is decorated.

Grounds are purely neutral greys, equal RGB, no warmth, with a very subtle top-to-bottom wash fixed to the viewport (Light #F7F7F6→#EFEFEE, Dark #6A6A6A→#616161 around the base values). One orange per theme, used for words in their active or hovered state and for the active-thumbnail outline. Never for backgrounds, rules, or decoration. Classic keeps its transcribed palette (#7E7E7E, #686868, #F48B2E).

Typography

Archivo regular, 14px, sentence case: all prose and nav links.

Archivo regular, 14px, caps, 0.06em: labels, categories, jump links

Archivo bold, 14px, caps, 0.06em: page titles, kickers

One size: 14px, and exactly two weights: regular and bold. No in-between weights. Hierarchy comes from case, tracking, and bold — never scale. Nav links share the footer voice: regular, sentence case, no tracking. Every uppercase run tracks at 0.06em. The wordmark is not type at all: the original logo artwork is applied as a CSS mask filled with the theme ink, so the mark stays pixel-faithful and recolors per theme. The studies use exactly one typeface. Body line-height 1.6; prose measure capped at 60ch.

Spacing

Three steps only. S = 8px for micro gaps (thumbnail gutters, stacked footer lines). M = 24px within a section (plate to contact sheet, grid gaps, category label to grid). L = 64px between chapters (title to page top, gallery to credits, chapter to chapter, footer clearance). The footer’s bottom offset mirrors the masthead’s top offset for window symmetry. Available as var(--s), var(--m), var(--l) in css/study.css. The 64px masthead and its centering paddings derive from the same constant.

Grid

Rows are 80% wide, 1080px max, always centered as a block and flush-left inside. Two column patterns: 1/3 + 2/3 (credits and description, firm chapters, contact) and the auto-fill tile grid, minmax(200px, 1fr), for the work overview. Images fill their frame with object-fit: cover; paired images share one aspect ratio (the firm page shows both images at natural aspect).

Components
  • Masthead: 64px, wordmark left and nav right sharing one baseline, 24px item gaps; hides on scroll down, returns on scroll up. Below 980px the nav becomes a flush-left row under the wordmark (no hamburger).
  • Project page: title, full-width plate flush left (66vh, contain), contact-sheet strip, credits 1/3 + description 2/3, prev/next, footer.
  • Work overview: WORK and the category jump links share the title baseline; tile grids by category; names turn orange on hover.
  • Footer: © / address / phone+email spread left-middle-right on one baseline; phone and email are live links, hover orange; line breaks only at phrase seams (the email never splits); stacks flush-left below 1000px; pinned to the viewport bottom on short pages.
  • Contact map: a custom-styled vector map (MapLibre + OpenFreeMap tiles, style JSON in site tokens) — theme-aware linework, orange dot, zoom and pan, no third-party chrome. The address itself links to the visitor’s maps app.
  • Theme switcher: bottom-right, a quiet word naming the active theme (footer voice, orange on hover); hover opens the three options with ground swatches.
Rules
  • Active or hovered words turn orange. No underlines.
  • No divider lines, ever. Separation is whitespace.
  • No borders or keylines on images.
  • No centered text.
  • No new font sizes, weights, colors, or spacing values without updating this page first.
